# Tropische vormgeving

Eigen uitwerking geïnspireerd op de sfeer en de hoekige figuren in de spelscreenshots van https://www.sumovolley.com/. De illustraties hieronder zijn nieuw gegenereerd met de ingebouwde imagegen-tool; de originele bestanden zijn ongewijzigd overgenomen.

- `images/tropical-beach-v2.png`: speelveld zonder spelers of paal. De paal en veldlijn worden in de game op de exacte botsingscoördinaten getekend.
- `images/sumo-roster-v2.png`: transparante 2×2 sprite-atlas. Links boven Koa, rechts boven Riku, links onder Nalu, rechts onder Mako. De game en personagekaarten gebruiken dezelfde illustraties.
- De oude kleurcodes blijven interne sleutels zodat de bestaande eigenschappen van de personages behouden blijven.

## Prompt strand

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: finished background plate for a portrait 2D beach volleyball game, aspect ratio 1:2 (1024x2048). Create an original tropical beach scene in a polished faceted low-poly 3D game-art style: broad angular shapes, clean flat-shaded planes, gentle warm sunlight, no black outlines, no photoreal texture. Palette: deep blue upper sky fading into pale aqua at the horizon, turquoise lagoon, muted jade palm fronds, warm cream and golden sand. Fixed composition for gameplay: top 54 percent mostly open readable blue sky with only a few quiet angular wisps at the sides; distant small faceted islands and ocean horizon around 56 percent down; turquoise water and thin pale surf from 56 to 64 percent; dry flat sandy playing area across the ENTIRE width from 66 to 80 percent down, clear of objects. Two large palms frame only the far left and far right edges, trunks must remain outside the central 80 percent of the image. Bottom 20 percent is attractive foreground sand with a few small polygonal shells, stones and palm shadows confined to the corners. Camera is eye level looking outward from a beach toward the sea, game backdrop with depth, NOT top down. The center and both player positions at x=27% and x=73%, y=74% must be unobstructed sand. Do not include any people, wrestlers, balls, net, poles, court markings, text, logo, frame or interface. Full bleed edge-to-edge illustration.

## Prompt personages

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one production-ready transparent PNG sprite atlas for a sumo beach volleyball game. Create ONE square image divided into a precise invisible 2 by 2 grid of equal square cells. In each cell is ONE different full-body friendly adult sumo wrestler, rendered in the SAME polished angular low-poly 3D game style: faceted polygonal skin, broad clean flat-shaded planes, chunky shapes, very few details, soft warm upper-left lighting, no drawn black outlines. All four stand in the SAME ready-to-play pose, knees slightly bent, feet apart, elbows bent, hands open beside torso, body and face oriented three-quarter toward the RIGHT, camera almost at eye level. Compact proportions, enormous round angular bellies, thick sturdy legs, small heads with Japanese topknots, barefoot, wearing opaque traditional mawashi fully covering the pelvis. TOP LEFT: Koa, warm tan skin, coral red mawashi and matching thin headband, dark angular topknot, heavy strong build, confident thick eyebrows. TOP RIGHT: Riku, light peach skin, golden yellow mawashi, dark swept topknot, slightly more nimble build, cheerful determined face. BOTTOM LEFT: Nalu, deep warm brown skin, turquoise teal mawashi, short dark topknot, calm smile, broad balanced build. BOTTOM RIGHT: Mako, medium warm skin, plum purple mawashi, salt-and-pepper angular topknot, small tidy dark goatee, sturdy veteran build. Layout constraints CRITICAL: each wrestler wholly contained within its own equal square cell, aligned identically, same visual scale and baseline; top of hair at 10 percent of cell height, soles at 90 percent; entire silhouette between 10 and 90 percent of cell width. Enough empty transparent spacing so separate crops never overlap. Each character occupies only its cell. No props, no balls, no background, no floor, no cast shadow, no text, labels, logos, grid lines, frame, or checkerboard. GENUINELY TRANSPARENT background with alpha channel, including between arms and body and between legs. Original character designs; not copies of existing game characters.
